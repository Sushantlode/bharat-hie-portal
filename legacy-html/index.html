Our vision

A technology partner for preventive healthcare

We support the national vision of the highest possible level of health and well-being for all, at all ages, by building the digital and village-level infrastructure for screening, early diagnosis and connected care.

Bharat HIE connects hospitals, doctors, pharmacies, labs, blood banks and NGOs on one Health Information Exchange. Citizens are screened early at the PHC/Sub-centre/Gram Panchayat and at home, high-risk cases are acted on within hours, and every record follows the citizen with consent.

Services involved

Same loop for every flagged citizen: Flagged → BPO call → KPO review → Command Centre → Consultation → Pharmacy / Lab → Follow-up. If the citizen is still red or yellow after follow-up, a new loop starts. Green citizens move to monthly monitoring.

For healthcare providers

  1. Register your facility Hospital, clinic, pharmacy, lab, blood bank or NGO, with licence number
  2. Connect your software Use Bharat HIE FHIR APIs or our ready connectors for common HMIS and LIS products
  3. Verification Documents and a test exchange in the sandbox
  4. Go live Receive e-Prescriptions, orders and referrals in your own system

Flag colours

Red BP ≥180/120 or <90, sugar ≥300 or <70, SpO₂ <90%, pulse >130 or <45
Yellow BP ≥140/90, sugar ≥200, SpO₂ 90–94%, pulse 101–130 or 45–54, BMI ≥30
Green All vitals in normal range, routine monitoring

Thresholds shown for the demo. Final rules are approved by the state clinical committee.
