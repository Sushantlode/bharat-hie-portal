About us

Vision & Mission

Bharat HIE Portal is a Health Information Exchange that connects every healthcare provider, so that care is preventive, predictive, accessible and digitally connected. It is designed to support the vision of the National Health Mission (NHM).

◎ Our vision

A healthier India, where every citizen is screened early, cared for close to home and never faces financial hardship.

We aim for the highest possible level of health and well-being for all, at all ages, through prevention, early diagnosis and universal access to quality care.

➜ Our mission
  • One card, one login. Every citizen gets one ABHA / eHS-linked health card that works with every connected provider.
  • Connect every provider. Hospitals, doctors, pharmacies, labs, blood banks and NGOs exchange records through open FHIR APIs.
  • Consent first. Records move only with the citizen's consent, through a consent manager aligned with ABDM.
  • Prevent before treat. Screening at PHC/Sub-centre/Gram Panchayat and at home, so risk is found early.
  • Act within hours. Red flags reach the BPO call centre, KPO clinical review and Command Centre the same day.
  • Care at the doorstep. e-Consultation, e-Prescription, medicine delivery and sample collection at home.
  • Data for decisions. Live dashboards for districts and the state, from screening to recovery.
  • Local jobs. Health workers, call-centre staff and technicians hired from the communities they serve.

National Health Mission (NHM), India

National vision

“Attainment of the highest possible level of health and well-being for all at all ages, through a preventive and promotive health care orientation in all developmental policies, and universal access to good quality health care services without anyone having to face financial hardship as a consequence.” National Health Policy 2017, as stated on the NHM website

NHM aims for universal access to equitable, affordable and quality healthcare that is accountable and responsive to people's needs, especially for vulnerable and underserved populations.

Seven national health priorities and how the portal supports them

1

Preventive healthcare

Early detection, screening and prevention of diseases.

Portal support: Screening at PHC/Sub-centre/Gram Panchayat and at home, with automatic red, yellow and green flags.

2

Primary healthcare

Accessible healthcare at village and community levels.

Portal support: ANM and ASHA workers register citizens, record vitals and refer only when needed.

3

Maternal & child health

Protecting mothers, newborns and children.

Portal support: Matrujyoti mother and child tracking, immunization records and follow-up reminders.

4

Communicable diseases

Prevention and control of infectious diseases.

Portal support: Disease surveillance (IDSP / IHIP), TB, malaria, dengue and outbreak alerts on one dashboard.

5

Non-communicable diseases

Screening for diabetes, hypertension, cancer and other chronic diseases.

Portal support: Continuous monitoring, e-Consultation and e-Prescription loops until the citizen is back to green.

6

Digital healthcare

Strengthening health information systems and technology.

Portal support: A consent-based Health Information Exchange on FHIR, aligned with ABDM, ABHA and HMIS.

7

Universal health coverage

Affordable healthcare without financial hardship.

Portal support: Scheme eligibility checks for PM-JAY and state schemes, medicines and sample collection at home.

Our position

Bharat HIE is a technology and infrastructure partner that supports NHM's stated vision of moving healthcare delivery towards prevention, prediction, access and digital connection. It works with existing national programmes. It is not a separate health scheme.

This page describes a potential programme alignment . It does not claim approval, endorsement or partnership by the National Health Mission, the Ministry of Health and Family Welfare or the Government of India.
